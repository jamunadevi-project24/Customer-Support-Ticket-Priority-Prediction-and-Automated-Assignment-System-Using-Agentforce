# Business Requirements

## Objective
Automate customer support ticket priority and assignment using Salesforce and Agentforce.

## Stakeholders
- Customer
- Support Agent
- Support Manager

## Requirements
- Create and store support tickets
- Classify tickets as High, Medium, or Low
- Automatically assign support agents
- Use Agentforce for ticket analysis
- Create tasks for High-priority tickets
- Provide reports and dashboards
