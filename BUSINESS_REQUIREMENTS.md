# Business Requirements

## Objective
Automate customer support ticket priority and assignment using Salesforce and Agentforce.

## Stakeholders
- Customer
- Support Agent
- Support Manager

## Requirements
- Create and store support tickets
- Classify tickets as High, Medium, or Low
- Automatically assign support agents
- Use Agentforce for ticket analysis
- Create tasks for High-priority tickets
- Provide reports and dashboards
## Project Scope

- Build a Salesforce ticket management system.
- Use Agentforce to classify and assign tickets automatically.
- Create tasks for high-priority tickets and provide ticket visibility.

## Objectives

- Reduce customer response time.
- Minimize manual work and improve team productivity.
- Ensure critical issues are handled first and improve customer satisfaction.
